Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Brass & Midnight is a single-player game published by Ahsen Serkan Usta. This policy covers the Android app and this website.

What the game collects

Nothing. There is no account, no login and no analytics. We run no server and never receive your personal data.

What stays on your device

Your saved game, your sound settings and your ledger are stored only on your device, in the app’s own storage. If Android backup is switched on, that data may be included in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your own account settings. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads, and only when you choose to watch one in exchange for a hint. These are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and use them to select ads. Google’s handling of that data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings, under Privacy. Buying the full story removes all ads, and the ads library is not started at all for players who have bought it.

Purchases

The optional in-app purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details, no card numbers and no address.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no fonts, scripts or images from third parties. The browser-playable demo stores your progress in your own browser and sends it nowhere. Your hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs; we do not read or use them.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data that Google holds, use the privacy controls in your Google account.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
