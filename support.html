Support

A one-person studio, so replies come from a human, usually within a few days.

Email support@example.com. It helps to include your device model, your Android version, and which act you were in.

Common questions

I bought the full story but it still shows as locked

Open the Menu inside the game and tap Restore. That re-checks your Google account. Make sure you are signed in to Google Play with the same account you bought with. If it still shows as locked after a minute, email us with the order number from your Google Play receipt.

I changed phones. Do I have to pay again?

No. The purchase belongs to your Google account. Install the game, open the Menu and tap Restore.

Will my saved game move to a new phone?

Saves are stored on the device and are included in Android backup, so a device-to-device transfer or a cloud restore usually carries them over. They are not tied to your Google account the way the purchase is, so a save can be lost if backup was off.

I am stuck on a puzzle

Tap Hint. The first tap nudges, the second pushes, and the third simply tells you the answer. You can also open the Ledger and spend a borrowed minute to get the answer with no ad at all.

The rewarded ad did not give me the hint

The hint only unlocks if the ad finishes. If it was closed early, or the network dropped, nothing is deducted — just try again, or use a minute from your ledger instead.

How do I turn the music off?

Menu, then the Sound switch, or the Music and Effects sliders.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Google Play, not by us. Within 48 hours of purchase you can request one from the Google Play order history. After that, email us and we will do what we can.

Reporting a bug

If something is broken, a screenshot and a description of what you did just before it happened is usually enough to find it. Bugs that block progress are fixed first.
